01 / SALONS & BEAUTY

Make the first
appointment easier.

Give visitors one clear place to see your services, location and opening hours, then send an appointment request for your team to confirm.

Illustrative journey

  1. A visitor checks your supplied service list and opening hours.
  2. They send a contact or appointment request through the website.
  3. Your team reviews availability and confirms directly.
  4. You use the plan’s fault ticket route if the hosted form stops working.

Solo salon example

One named user on Micro is R49/month, plus R1,490 once for Micro Launch. The template page can have up to five sections and ten listed services. Domain and email supplier charges are separate.

Micro covers hosted platform faults. Service-menu edits, personal device support and a booking engine with live availability are separately scoped. For supported accounts and a primary computer, compare Essential.

02 / ESTATE AGENCIES

Your agency.
One supported workspace.

Present your agency, routes for buyer and seller enquiries, and the people to contact. Support each agent’s agreed cloud account and primary computer as your team changes.

Illustrative journey

  1. A seller reads the agency’s public services and sends an enquiry.
  2. A named team member receives it through the agreed route.
  3. A new agent joins using the plan’s joiner checklist and allowance.
  4. The team raises a support request when an agreed account or primary computer needs attention.

Four-person agency example

Four users on Essential are R1,596/month, plus R4,990 once for Essential Launch. This covers up to five template pages and one supported workspace. The business shares 120 minutes of assisted support/month with no rollover.

Up to two joiner/leaver events and one standard 15-minute website edit per business/month use that time allowance. Property-portal feeds, CRM migrations, listing imports and new listing software need a separate scope.

03 / INDEPENDENT PRACTICES

Public information.
Organised administration.

Help people find your practice and send a public appointment request. Keep the IT engagement focused on the business website, supported accounts and administrative devices.

Illustrative journey

  1. A visitor reads the practitioner information and contact details you supply.
  2. They send an appointment request with basic contact details.
  3. Your team confirms availability directly using its agreed process.
  4. Administrative staff use their supported accounts and raise IT requests through the agreed channel.

Three-person practice example

Three users on Care are R1,797/month, plus R7,990 once for Care Onboarding. The business shares 180 minutes assisted support/month, with monthly primary-device patch-status reviews and daily website backups retaining 14 copies.

Coverage is for business IT and administration. Medical records, clinical systems, claims, clinical decisions and compliance certification are outside this service. Appointment requests should contain basic contact details and scheduling needs. Keep patient and clinical information out of public forms.

04 / OTHER SERVICE BUSINESSES

Connect the steps
behind your service.

Consultancies, repair businesses, agencies and other service teams can start with a clear website, supported accounts and an agreed handover between enquiries and the people doing the work.

Illustrative journey

  1. A visitor understands the service and sends an enquiry.
  2. Your team reviews it using the agreed workspace.
  3. An existing template workflow routes agreed information between the supported steps.
  4. Scheduled workflow time and a quarterly technology review help you make planned changes within scope.

Six-person service team example

Six users on Operations are R5,394/month, plus R11,990 once for Operations Onboarding. Setup can include up to eight template pages and one existing template workflow with up to five steps and agreed inputs.

The plan includes two scheduled hours per business/month for changes to agreed existing template workflows, with no rollover. New applications, custom code, major migrations, SMS/WhatsApp messaging and supplier costs are quoted separately.
