01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

From first conversation
to signed project.

Find salons, estate agencies, independent practices and other service businesses that need fixed-scope setup and recurring IT coverage. Bring our team into qualified conversations to work through the technical scope and help move each opportunity forward.

What you’ll do

Identify prospects, start conversations with decision-makers and learn how their teams work today. Keep track of active opportunities, coordinate introductions and follow up as proposals move towards a decision.

What you’ll bring

Experience selling to businesses, confidence discussing operational problems, and a way to build a pipeline independently. Experience selling software or IT services in South Africa is valuable.

How commission works

Commission is tied to qualifying client deals. The written agreement will set out the rate, how it’s calculated and when it’s paid, subject to applicable South African requirements.

How we’ll work together

You lead prospecting and the sales relationship. Our software engineers and business analysts help assess requirements, shape proposals and answer technical questions.
