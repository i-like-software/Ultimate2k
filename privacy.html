ULTIMATE2K / PRIVACY

Your enquiry and your information.

This page explains how the public Ultimate2k website handles an enquiry. Client IT systems and employee access are covered by the separate service agreement agreed during onboarding.

Forms and email

The enquiry and careers forms prepare a draft in your browser. You choose when to send it through your email provider. When you email info@ultimate2k.co.za, we receive the information you include and use it to respond, assess your requirements or consider your application. Share information relevant to that purpose.

Browser storage and demonstrations

The site can remember your motion preference for the browser session. Interactive demos use fictional data. Treat any information you type into a demo as a local demonstration and keep personal or confidential records out of it.

Website hosting

Our hosting provider processes technical request data to deliver and secure the website. Following an external link or opening your email app uses the relevant provider’s service and privacy terms.

Keeping and accessing correspondence

We keep correspondence while it is needed for the enquiry, recruitment process or resulting business relationship, and for applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact info@ultimate2k.co.za to ask about information you have sent, request a correction or ask about deletion.
