# Reference research and design decisions

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Read the live navigation and available content; captured live desktop views of BBD, Digital Platforms and Tiago Fragoso, and inspected One Page Love’s archived ComicKult screenshot. Some Digital Platforms subpages returned rate limits; their existence and grouping were verified in the live primary navigation. XML sitemap endpoints were unavailable, so the map below uses the actual visible navigation, not an assumed XML inventory.

## References

- [Digital Platforms](https://www.digitalplatforms.co.za/): full business site hierarchy, a visual service-led hero, distinct service pages, work and enquiry flow. Read available custom software, design/development, integration, app, SEO, team and contact pages. About, work, service overview, e-commerce, social marketing and news pages were attempted; some were rate-limited or timed out.
- [BBD](https://bbdsoftware.com/): enterprise positioning, full-width opening, clear services hierarchy and separation of engineering, enablement and support. [Project approach](https://bbdsoftware.com/project-approach/) informed the need for a clear delivery narrative. Ultimate2k’s copy does not claim BBD’s scale, clients, certifications or support SLAs.
- [ComicKult reference](https://onepagelove.com/comickult): tiled waterfall of covers, a strong readable foreground and small interaction details. Original site is offline/redesigned per its reference page; inspected the preserved screenshot. Reinterpreted as original geometric software covers in the comic edition, without using licensed comic characters or covers.
- [Tiago Fragoso reference](https://onepagelove.com/tiago-fragoso) and [live site](https://tiagofragoso.com/): editorial pacing, frames and personality. Reinterpreted as straight geometric panels and print textures, avoiding hand lettering and doodles.

## Two directions

Professional: spacious white foundation, deep logo red, measured typography, architectural system illustration, modest movement.

Comic: full expressive treatment, animated original cover wall, condensed headlines, hard offset shadows, numbered panels, halftones, sharper content framing. Long-form reading stays on white; dark/red sections are used for the hero and closing call to action.

The brand mark is the user-supplied `ItLooksSoNice.png`. `assets/logo.png` is a 128px web-sized copy with the same design; the full-resolution original is retained separately in the local deliverables. Geometric illustrations and concept UI artwork are original HTML/CSS/SVG. Fonts: DM Sans and Barlow Condensed, locally hosted under SIL OFL.
