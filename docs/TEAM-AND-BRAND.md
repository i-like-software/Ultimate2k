The site uses web-optimised copies, CSS grayscale and individual face-focused crops in 4:5 frames. Images scale uniformly; they are not stretched to fill the frames.

`assets/brand/u2k-logo.svg` is a clean, editable vector reconstruction of the supplied U2K raster logo. The crimson background, U, 2 and K are separate named shapes. It uses a square canvas without the original image’s outer black padding. Header, hero and footer images reference this SVG.

The favicon set includes root `favicon.svg`, multi-size root `favicon.ico`, PNGs at 16/32/48/180/192/512px, an Apple touch icon and `site.webmanifest`. Links are versioned to refresh older cached favicons. The logo can be edited in an SVG-capable vector editor and exported at any size.

The complete reusable brand kit is also retained as a separate local deliverable, including transparent black and white variants and editing instructions.
