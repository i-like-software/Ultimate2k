# Before publication

- Choose the professional, comic, or an agreed blend of the two directions.
- Approve the proposed service descriptions and confirm SEO/social media as actual offerings.
- Supply a real contact email, phone/location if desired, and an enquiry destination. The current form only produces a local brief.
- Supply approved company description, team information, real portfolio items and any permission to show client names/logos.
- Supply real news and career opportunities, or decide to omit those pages at launch.
- Supply the applicable B-BBEE certificate, PAIA manual, usage/privacy terms and feedback channel; placeholder pages claim no certification or legal status.
- Confirm any registration marks before using them. The supplied logo has not been altered; website wordmarks do not add a registration claim.
- Approve the production homepage and remove review navigation/noindex metadata before enabling Pages.

The review versions intentionally avoid invented business evidence. They can be assessed now without final company content.
