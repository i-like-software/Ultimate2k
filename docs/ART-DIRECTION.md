## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: static full-bleed website hero background, wide landscape 16:9. Create a densely tiled wall of 30-40 ORIGINAL vintage comic-book covers, like a collector's comic-cover wall photographed perfectly front-on. Narrow portrait covers in staggered vertical columns, edge-to-edge with tiny black gaps, diverse distinct covers, some slight misalignment, no blank space and no foreground website interface. Authentic detailed drawn comic illustrations, dramatic inked human figures, retro-futurist cityscapes, masked original adventurers, robots, space explorers, noir detectives, machinery and cosmic adventures, screenprint halftones and subtle paper print texture. Restrained palette strictly crimson red, black, warm white, and greys only. Every cover looks like a real printed comic book, with expressive original illustrations, bold tiny fictional mastheads and issue labels, NOT flat corporate icons or repeated geometric diagrams. Composition dense across the whole image. No existing licensed comic characters, no real publisher marks, no brands, no watermarks, no website headlines, no large central text. Keep the illustration reasonably bright before a dark CSS overlay is applied later. This is decorative backdrop art for Ultimate2k, a professional enterprise software solutions devhouse. It must feel unmistakably like a wall of comic books, sophisticated and graphic, and it will remain stationary.
