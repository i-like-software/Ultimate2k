What gets a separate quote?

Extra assisted support is R350/hour, billed in 15-minute increments after your approval. Monthly assisted support and workflow time have no rollover.

Separate work includes major migrations, new software, onsite work, network/server builds, hardware repair, SEO campaigns, social posting, bulk data entry and SMS/WhatsApp messaging. Supplier charges are agreed before purchase. We approve any overage together before billing it.

Micro includes hosted-service fault help. Personal device/app troubleshooting, content edits and changes beyond the original setup receive a quote first.

How payment and cancellation work

Monthly service is paid in advance. Cancel before your next billing date and your current paid month runs to its end, with no future billing. Active seats are adjusted prospectively.

Setup is paid 50% before work and 50% after the agreed scope is approved, before launch. We give 30 days’ notice of future rate changes so you can decide whether to renew.

Your domain and supplier subscriptions remain in your ownership. Before hosting ends, we arrange a secure export of standard website content and your account register. Bespoke migration work is quoted separately.

Before you choose a plan

Can one person use a plan?

Yes. One active user can subscribe, and there is no minimum monthly account fee. Micro covers 1–5 users. Essential, Care and Operations support 1–50 users on the standard cloud stack. A shared inbox does not add a seat, and each supported person needs their own account and credentials.

What happens if I add or remove users?

We adjust the subscription prospectively for the active named users. One primary computer per supported user is covered on managed plans. The Micro website stays active while at least one paid user remains. Hosting ends when the subscription ends, so we arrange any export before the paid period finishes.

How does pooled support time work?

Each user contributes the plan’s monthly time allowance to a business-wide pool. Four Essential users provide 120 minutes/month. Joiner/leaver events, website edits and training use that allowance and also have the plan-specific limits shown above. Unused assisted support and workflow hours do not roll over. Extra time is R350/hour in 15-minute increments after approval.

Does the service fee include email and Microsoft or Google licences?

Business-email mailboxes, Microsoft/Google or other workspace licences, domain fees and supplier subscriptions are separate. Managed plans include administration of one supported cloud workspace. Essential Launch includes creating up to 10 new accounts in that workspace. Old email and data migrations receive a separate quote.

What does device care cover?

Care and Operations include a monthly patch-status review and agreed updates for each supported primary computer, plus an inventory of those devices. Endpoint security tools, endpoint protection licences and mailbox-backup tools are separately priced if required. Service is remote and delivered during published staffed hours.

Can an appointment-request form manage patient records?

For independent practices, these plans cover business IT, administration and public appointment requests. The form should collect only the details needed to request a time. Clinical records, medical decisions, claims and hospital or clinical-system support require a different scope. Please keep clinical patient information out of public forms.

Will you set up a Google Business Profile and improve search ranking?

Essential Launch and higher include assistance for businesses eligible for Google Business Profile, subject to Google verification. Basic search setup covers page titles, descriptions and indexing setup. Search campaigns, ongoing SEO and custom content production are separately scoped. Search position depends on Google and other factors.

Can overseas businesses subscribe?

We provide remote service internationally with agreed time-zone coverage. The published Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 SAST service hours apply until we contract a different arrangement. All listed service fees are in South African rand.

Can you build something beyond the plans?

Yes. We separately scope custom applications, integrations, complex environments, additional brands/sites and major migrations. Operations includes changes to an agreed existing template workflow. New applications and custom code receive a project quote. Explore our custom software capabilities.
