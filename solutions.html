01 / Get your business online

Business Online Starter Pack

Customers need somewhere to find you, see what you offer and get in touch. Choose a fixed-scope website launch, with account setup and staff support available on the managed plans.

What you get

  • Micro Launch includes one template page, one form and one domain connection.
  • Essential Launch adds up to five pages, basic indexing setup and up to ten new workspace accounts. Email supplier charges are separate.
  • Eligible Google Business Profile assistance starts with Essential Launch. A full booking engine or catalogue is scoped and priced separately.
Fixed setup from R1,490

Monthly plans start at R49 per user. See pricing for support allowances, fixed setup and separately billed supplier costs.

02 / Find company information

Company Knowledge Hub

When a decision is buried in WhatsApp and the supporting document is in someone’s inbox, work slows down. The hub gives staff one place to search the sources they can access and see where an answer came from.

What you get

  • Search across connected messages, email, documents and CRM records.
  • A shared calendar and dashboards for teams and managers.
  • Optional mobile access and a WhatsApp assistant, scoped to your organisation.
From R30,000

Larger deployments can reach seven figures. The number of systems, access rules and reporting needs shape the quote.

03 / Organise your hiring

Applicant Tracking System

Give your hiring team one place to receive applications, review candidates and see who needs a response. We shape the screening fields and hiring stages around how you recruit.

  • A hiring pipeline your team manages from review to offer.
