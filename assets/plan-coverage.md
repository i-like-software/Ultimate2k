# Ultimate2k plans and service coverage

Launch service specification · 11 November 2026 · All amounts in South African rand

## Prices and who each plan covers

| Plan | Monthly service fee | Fixed setup fee | Standard team size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Micro | R49 per user | R1,490 | 1–5 users |
| Essential | R399 per user | R4,990 | 1–50 users |
| Care | R599 per user | R7,990 | 1–50 users |
| Operations | R899 per user | R11,990 | 1–50 users |

There is no minimum monthly account fee. One user can subscribe. A user is a named person with their own supported account. Shared mailboxes do not create extra seats. Shared passwords cannot be used to avoid named seats. Each subscription covers one business, brand, location configuration and website. Monthly fees change prospectively with active users. Listed service fees cover the stated scope. Agreed third-party charges are separate.

## Micro

- One hosted template website with one page and a contact or appointment-request form.
- Shared site limits of 1 GB storage and 10 GB monthly transfer per business.
- HTTPS, monthly supported-stack updates and weekly backups with four retained copies.
- Self-service guides and tickets for faults affecting the hosted service. First-response target of two business days.
- Content edits, device support, business-app troubleshooting and email mailbox hosting are separately priced. Domain registration and renewals are separate.
- At least one active paid user keeps the site hosted. Arrange content export before the final subscription ends.

Micro Launch includes up to five page sections, ten listed services, customer-supplied content and branding, one form, one domain connection, one revision round and a 30-minute handover. It excludes data migration. Target delivery is 5–10 business days after complete content and access are supplied, subject to the agreed schedule.

## Essential

- One website with up to five pages, 5 GB storage and 50 GB monthly transfer per business.
- Administration of one agreed cloud workspace and remote troubleshooting of one primary supported computer per user.
- Accounts, password and MFA administration, shared mailbox settings, a licence register and renewal reminders.
- 30 minutes of assisted support per user each month, pooled across the business, with no rollover.
- Up to two employee joining/leaving events per business each month within the support allowance.
- One standard website content edit of up to 15 minutes per business each month within that allowance.
- Monthly website updates and weekly backups with four retained copies. First-response target of one business day.
- Device monitoring, endpoint protection licences and workspace-mailbox backups are outside this plan.

Essential Launch includes up to five template pages, supplied content, one enquiry form, one domain connection, basic titles/descriptions/indexing setup, eligible Google Business Profile assistance, up to ten new accounts in one workspace, two revision rounds and a 60-minute handover. Existing email/data migrations are separately quoted. Google approval and search position are outside our control.

## Care

Includes Essential coverage, with the following replacements and additions.

- 60 minutes of assisted support per user each month, pooled, with no rollover.
- Up to five joining/leaving events per business each month within that allowance.
- Up to 30 minutes of website edits per business each month within that allowance.
- A 30-minute team training session per business each quarter within the support allowance.
- Monthly patch-status review and agreed updates for the supported primary devices, plus a device inventory.
- Daily website backups with 14 retained copies. First-response target of four business hours.
- Endpoint protection and workspace backup tooling are separately priced where required.

Care Onboarding includes Essential Launch plus inventory and enrolment of up to ten existing supported computers, a baseline MFA/access and patch-status review, a joining/leaving checklist and a supplier/licence register. This is an operational setup and does not certify security or regulatory compliance.

## Operations

Includes Care coverage, with the following replacements and additions.

- 90 minutes of assisted support per user each month, pooled, with no rollover.
- Up to ten joining/leaving events per business each month within that allowance.
- Up to 60 minutes of website edits per business each month within that allowance.
- A one-hour team training session per business each quarter within that allowance.
- Two scheduled hours per business each month for changes to agreed existing template workflows, with no rollover.
- A 45-minute quarterly technology and licence review.
- Up to eight website pages, 10 GB storage and 100 GB monthly transfer per business.
- First-response target of two business hours. New software builds are quoted separately.

Operations Onboarding includes Care Onboarding, up to eight template pages, one existing template workflow of up to five steps using supported tools, and a 90-minute handover. The included workflow uses configuration rather than custom code.

## Fixed setup additions

- Above ten users/devices in a managed setup, add R250 once per additional supported user/device pair. A pair covers one person and one computer.
- An extra website page using the existing template and supplied content costs R450 once, subject to the subscription page limit or an agreed plan change.
- Additional brands, location configurations, integrations and migrations receive a separate scope and price before work starts.

## Support hours and boundaries

Support is remote, Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 SAST, excluding South African public holidays. These hours also apply overseas unless agreed otherwise. Response targets measure the first human response during those hours. Resolution depends on the issue and any supplier involvement. Higher-priority outages are triaged first. There is no published guaranteed resolution or uptime commitment.

Assisted support above the included allowance is R350 per hour in 15-minute increments, after approval. Website edits, joining/leaving events and training draw from the stated support allowance. Operations workflow work has its own two-hour allowance. We agree timing and requirements for employee changes before scheduling them.

Hardware, onsite work, servers, network rebuilds, major migrations, new applications, social media posting, advertising, SEO campaigns, bulk data entry, SMS/WhatsApp messaging and supplier fees are separately priced. Domain, email, premium software and security/backup subscriptions are itemised before purchase. We use an open-source-first supported stack where it fits the work.

Independent practice coverage concerns business IT, public websites and appointment requests. Clinical records, diagnosis, claims processing, medical devices and compliance certification require separate specialist scope. Public enquiry forms must not collect clinical or sensitive health information.

## Billing and exit

Subscriptions are paid monthly in advance. Cancel before the next billing date to stop renewal; access continues until the paid period ends. Setup is 50% before work starts and 50% after approval of the agreed scope, before launch. Scope changes and overages require approval first.

Domains and third-party subscriptions remain in the client's ownership. Standard website content and the account register can be handed over through an agreed secure process. Additional migration labour is separately quoted. Hosting ends when the subscription ends, so arrange export before that date. Future rate changes are notified at least 30 days ahead and the client can decline renewal.

## Choosing a plan

Choose Micro for a maintained digital starting point with self-service tools. Choose Essential for routine administration and staff troubleshooting. Care adds device upkeep and a larger support allowance. Operations adds scheduled workflow improvements and regular planning. Larger or unusual environments receive an agreed custom scope.
