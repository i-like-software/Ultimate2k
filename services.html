Build from there.

Website storage is 1 GB with 10 GB monthly transfer shared per business.

Fault ticket first-response target is two business days. Content edits, business-email mailboxes and personal device or app support are outside Micro.

02 / FIXED SETUP

A clear starting scope.

Each fixed setup covers one business, one brand and one location. Setup is paid 50% before work and 50% after the agreed scope is approved, before launch. We price additions before starting them.

Micro Launch · R1,490 once

One template page with up to five sections, supplied logo, text and images, up to ten listed services, one contact or appointment-request form, one domain connection, one revision round and a 30-minute owner handover. Target delivery is 5–10 business days after complete content and access are supplied.

Essential Launch · R4,990 once

Up to five template pages, supplied content, one enquiry form, one domain connection, page titles, descriptions and indexing setup. Includes eligible Google Business Profile assistance subject to verification, up to ten new accounts in one workspace, two revision rounds and a 60-minute handover. Old email and data migrations are separately scoped.

Care Onboarding · R7,990 once

Essential Launch plus inventory and enrolment of up to ten supported existing computers, a baseline MFA, access and patch-status review, a join/leave checklist and a documented provider and licence register. This is a practical IT baseline review.

Operations Onboarding · R11,990 once

Care Onboarding plus up to eight template pages and configuration of one existing template workflow with up to five steps using supported tools and agreed inputs. Includes a 90-minute handover. Custom code is scoped as a separate project.

Above ten users/devices in a managed setup, an additional supported user/device pair with one computer costs R250 once. An extra website page using the same template and supplied content costs R450 setup, subject to the selected plan’s page limit or an agreed plan change. Extra sites, brands and location configurations need a custom scope.

03 / SUPPORT THAT IS DEFINED

Know what your plan covers.

Our remote service hours are Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 SAST, excluding South African public holidays. These hours apply internationally until different coverage is agreed. First-response timings are targets during staffed hours. Higher-priority outages are triaged first, and investigation determines the resolution plan.

Extra assisted support is R350/hour, billed in 15-minute increments after approval. Projects, onsite work, network/server builds, hardware repair, major migrations, SEO campaigns, social posting, bulk data entry, SMS/WhatsApp messaging, new software and supplier fees are separately scoped.

Monthly fees are paid in advance. Cancel before your next billing date and your current paid month runs to its end. Domain and third-party subscriptions stay in your ownership. Arrange an export of standard website content and the account register before hosting ends. Bespoke migration labour is quoted separately.
