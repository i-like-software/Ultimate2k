Present your practice.

Organise staff accounts.

Business IT, with a clear boundary.

What do setup packages include?

Micro Launch — R1,490: one template page with up to five sections and ten listed services, supplied content/logo/images, one request form, one domain connection, one revision and a 30-minute handover. No migration. Target 5–10 business days after complete content and access; timing is confirmed, not guaranteed.

Essential Launch — R4,990: up to five template pages, one form and domain connection, basic page titles/descriptions/indexing setup, eligible Google Business Profile assistance, up to ten new accounts in one workspace, two revisions and a 60-minute handover. Google verification is subject to Google; search rankings are not guaranteed. No old-email or data migration.

Care Onboarding — R7,990: Essential Launch plus inventory and enrolment of up to ten supported existing computers, an access/multi-factor sign-in and computer-update review, a staff join/leave checklist and a provider/licence register.

Operations Onboarding — R11,990: Care Onboarding plus up to eight template pages, one existing supported template workflow with up to five steps and a 90-minute handover. No custom code.

Managed setups above ten supported user/device pairs add R250 once per additional pair. Additional template pages cost R450 once, subject to the selected plan’s page limit or an agreed plan change. Other additions are scoped before work.

How much help is included each month?

Essential: up to two staff join/leave events and one standard 15-minute website edit per business/month. Care: up to five events and 30 minutes of website edits/month, plus 30 minutes of training/quarter. Operations: up to ten events and 60 minutes of edits/month, plus one hour of training/quarter. These tasks all draw from the plan’s pooled support allowance; they are not extra time on top.

Care and Essential website limits are 5 GB storage / 50 GB monthly transfer per business; Operations is 10 GB / 100 GB. Operations also includes a 45-minute quarterly technology/licence review. Micro has platform-fault help rather than an assisted support allowance.

Unused support and workflow hours do not roll over. Extra help is R350/hour in 15-minute increments, with your approval. All support is remote: Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00 SAST, excluding South African public holidays. Response targets are first replies during these hours, not guaranteed resolution or uptime.

What costs extra, and can we cancel?

Domain registration/renewal, mailboxes, software licences, security/backup tools and other supplier subscriptions are separate. Major migrations, hardware, onsite work, networks/servers, new apps, live booking engines, ongoing SEO and messaging fees need a separate quote. You keep ownership of your domain and accounts.

Monthly service is paid in advance; cancel before the next billing date and the paid month runs to its end. Setup is 50% before work and 50% after scope approval, before launch. We give 30 days’ notice of future rate changes. Hosting ends with the subscription; arrange export before the paid period ends. Bespoke migration work is quoted separately.

Not sure which plan fits? Choose “Help me choose” in the enquiry. We confirm the supported tools, staff count and full scope before you commit.
