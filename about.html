A PRACTICAL PLACE TO START

Understand the work.
Support the tools.

A small team may need a clear website and a place for enquiries before it needs a complex platform. As it grows, account access, employee changes and everyday IT issues become part of the working week. We offer a defined starting scope and monthly coverage that can grow with active users.

We favour open-source tools where they fit the requirements and can be operated responsibly by our team. We assess paid tools when the business needs them and agree supplier costs before purchase.

Our launch focus is South African small businesses. All services are remote, with international work using the published SAST service hours until a different arrangement is agreed.

HOW WE WORK

Scope it.
Set it up. Support it.

One agreed scope makes the work easier to review. Changes and supplier costs are discussed before work proceeds.

01

Understand

Walk through your business, current tools, users and the outcome you need. Check the support boundaries and select a starting plan.

02

Agree

Confirm the fixed setup, content, access, supplier charges and responsibilities. Setup starts after the first 50% payment.

03

Set up

Configure the agreed website and supported accounts. Review the work together, complete the included revisions and hand over the agreed scope before launch.

04

Support

Use the plan’s fault ticket or assisted support coverage during staffed hours. Agree extra work before it starts and review changes as your team grows.

A WORKING RELATIONSHIP

Clear ownership.
Visible decisions.

Monthly plans can start with one active user. Supplier accounts and the domain belong to your business, and the account register helps keep responsibilities visible.

Your accounts

Your domain and third-party subscriptions stay in your ownership. We use the agreed access needed to deliver the service and document supported providers and licences where included.

Defined support

Service hours are Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 SAST, excluding South African public holidays. First-response targets describe when we aim to respond during staffed hours. The issue determines the work required to resolve it.

Changes priced first

A fixed setup has a fixed scope. Additions, complex environments and new applications are assessed separately. Extra assisted support costs R350/hour in approved 15-minute increments.

A clear handover

On exit, arrange secure export of standard website content and the account register before the paid hosting period ends. Any bespoke migration labour is quoted before work begins.
